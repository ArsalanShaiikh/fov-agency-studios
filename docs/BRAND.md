# Bombay Craft: Brand Vision & Website Direction

The source of truth for the website's positioning, copy and design decisions.

- **Working brand name:** Bombay Craft
- **Brand category:** Digital-first creative and technology studio
- **Core positioning:** Websites, digital products, and business technology, supported by design, content, and production.
- **Born in Bombay. Crafted for the world.**

Bombay Craft brings creativity, design, and technology together to help businesses build a stronger presence, work smarter, and grow with confidence. From a first website for a small business to a custom CRM or digital platform for a larger organization, we create useful, thoughtful digital solutions around real business needs.

## 1. The Big Idea

We are one creative and technology partner for many business needs.

Bombay Craft is built on the belief that businesses should not have to coordinate multiple disconnected teams to bring an idea to life. We connect strategy, design, development, and creative production under one roof.

Technology is our foundation. Websites and digital products are the centre of our work. Branding, video, photography, and marketing help bring those digital experiences to life and help our clients reach the right people.

We want to serve both growing small businesses and established organizations, with the same care, curiosity, and commitment to quality.

## 2. Vision

To make thoughtful technology and creative expertise accessible to every ambitious business, while building an organization that creates positive impact beyond itself.

We envision Bombay Craft becoming a trusted, globally minded studio born in Bombay: a place where good ideas become useful digital experiences, businesses get the tools they need to grow, and success creates opportunities for people and communities beyond our team.

Our ambition is not only to build a successful company. It is to build a company whose work, opportunities, and future contributions benefit others too.

## 3. Mission

To help businesses of every size grow through well-crafted websites, intuitive digital experiences, and practical technology, supported by clear strategy, strong design, and creative storytelling.

We aim to:

- Build websites that are fast, accessible, useful, and designed around business goals.
- Create custom web applications, CRM systems, dashboards, and workflows that solve real operational problems.
- Make complex technology easier to understand and use.
- Bring design, development, and creative production together in one collaborative process.
- Build lasting client relationships through transparency, reliability, and thoughtful execution.
- Grow a socially responsible business that contributes time, skills, resources, and support to community initiatives as we become able to do so.

## 5. Who We Serve

- **Small businesses and local brands** that need a professional digital presence.
- **Startups and founders** turning an idea into a product or service.
- **Growing companies** that need better systems, automation, or custom applications.
- **Established and enterprise businesses** that need reliable digital experiences, integrations, and operational tools.
- **Community organizations and NGOs** that may benefit from accessible digital support as our social-impact work develops.

Our approach should adapt to each client's scale and needs. We do not believe that a smaller business deserves less thoughtful work.

## 6. Our Values

- **Craft over shortcuts.** We care about the details, the quality of the experience, and whether the solution genuinely works.
- **Technology with purpose.** We use technology to solve problems, not simply to make something look advanced.
- **People before ego.** We listen, collaborate, explain decisions, and treat clients, teammates, partners, and communities with respect.
- **Accessible ambition.** We want high-quality digital work to be within reach for businesses at different stages of growth.
- **One team, shared ownership.** We bring disciplines together and take responsibility for the work from idea to delivery.
- **Honest partnerships.** We communicate clearly about scope, cost, timelines, trade-offs, and outcomes.
- **Growth that gives back.** We want our success to create benefits beyond the company. Over time, we aim to support NGOs and community initiatives through donations, pro-bono work, skills, partnerships, and other responsible contributions that are realistic for our capacity.

## 7. Our Social-Impact Commitment

Social impact is part of the long-term vision, not a marketing claim to make before the work exists.

As Bombay Craft grows, we intend to develop a meaningful community-support program. Possible directions include:

- Offering selected website or digital services to NGOs at reduced cost or pro bono.
- Supporting community organizations with digital literacy and practical technology.
- Donating a defined portion of profits when financially sustainable.
- Partnering with credible organizations working on causes aligned with our values.
- Creating opportunities for people to learn, contribute, and develop their skills.

We will start with achievable commitments, communicate them honestly, and share real outcomes when initiatives are in place. **We should never claim partnerships, donations, or impact figures that have not actually happened.**

## 8. Brand Personality & Voice

Bombay Craft should feel modern, capable, creative, approachable, grounded (proud of its Bombay roots while working to international standards) and purposeful.

Use clear, confident, human language. Explain technical services in terms of the problems they solve. Be warm without becoming overly casual, and ambitious without making exaggerated promises. Avoid generic claims such as "we do everything better" or "the number-one agency." Show the breadth of our capabilities through clear service descriptions and real work.

## 9. Website Strategy

The website should immediately communicate that Bombay Craft is a digital-first creative and technology studio, with websites and technology as its strongest offering.

**Goals:** make the core services easy to understand; build trust through strong design, clear explanations, and real project work; help visitors identify the right service; generate qualified enquiries; show we can support clients from an initial idea to a complete digital solution; introduce people-first values and the long-term social-impact commitment without distracting from the commercial offering.

**Suggested structure:** Home, Services, Websites, UI/UX & Digital Products, CRM & Automation, Creative Studio, Work / Case Studies, About, Impact, Contact. The navigation can be simplified at launch. Do not create empty pages merely to fill the menu; publish sections when there is useful content to support them.

## 10. Homepage Messaging

- **Hero headline:** Digital experiences. Thoughtfully crafted.
- **Supporting copy:** We design and build websites, digital products, and business systems that help organizations work smarter and grow. From UI/UX and custom development to CRM and automation, Bombay Craft brings creativity and technology together under one roof.
- **Primary CTA:** Start a project. **Secondary CTA:** Explore our work.
- Alternatives: "From bold ideas to better digital experiences." / "Your ideas, built for what comes next." / "Websites and technology built around your business." / "One creative partner. A world of possibilities." Choose one primary message rather than presenting every tagline at once.

## 11. Visual Direction

Premium, modern, expressive, and distinctly connected to Bombay, without relying on predictable city symbols or decorative luxury clichés.

- Make typography the main brand asset.
- Use confident, editorial layouts with generous breathing room.
- Give project imagery and real interfaces room to stand out.
- Use restrained motion to support the experience, not slow it down.
- Make the website feel technology-led, with creative production as a supporting layer.
- Build a responsive system that remains clear and usable on mobile.

**Palette:** deep wine / oxblood (primary), warm ivory, charcoal / near-black, and a restrained metallic or muted accent used sparingly.

**Typography and local character:** an ownable wordmark with expressive lettering and a subtle Marathi or Indian typographic influence where it feels natural. Contemporary and legible; cultural references should never feel like decoration.

**Motion:** purposeful transitions, refined scroll animation, carefully selected project showcases. Animation should improve storytelling and navigation rather than compete with content or performance.

## 12. How We Work

1. **Discover:** understand the business, audience, goals, constraints, and current problems.
2. **Define:** agree on scope, priorities, deliverables, timeline, and measures of success.
3. **Design:** plan the experience, structure, interface, and visual direction.
4. **Build:** develop, integrate, and test the solution.
5. **Launch:** prepare the site or system for real-world use.
6. **Improve:** provide agreed support, measure performance where possible, and identify the next improvements.

The process can adapt to project size, but clients should always understand what happens next.

## 13. What Makes Bombay Craft Different

- **Digital-first thinking:** websites and technology are central, not an afterthought.
- **Connected capabilities:** design, development, business systems, and creative production can work together.
- **Business-aware solutions:** we consider the operational goal behind the brief, not just the visual output.
- **Flexible collaboration:** we can support a focused project or a broader digital roadmap.
- **A wider purpose:** we want to build a sustainable business that contributes positively beyond its immediate clients and team.

These are principles to prove through projects, process, relationships, and results, not just statements on a website.

## 14. Long-Term Direction

1. **Build trust:** a strong portfolio, a clear website-development offering, reliable delivery, a recognizable brand.
2. **Expand digital capability:** web applications, CRM, integrations, automation, ongoing support; repeatable processes and stronger case studies.
3. **Build an integrated studio:** connect strategy, design, development, branding, marketing, and production into one client experience.
4. **Make community impact measurable:** sustainable partnerships and a clear approach to pro-bono work, donations, or community technology support, reported transparently.

The phases are directional, not promises tied to fixed dates.

## 15. Principles for Future Decisions

Does this help clients solve a real problem? Does it strengthen our digital-first focus? Can we deliver it well and responsibly? Is the experience clear and accessible? Does it respect the people involved? Can we explain the value honestly? Does it support a sustainable business and our wider purpose? If not, it may not belong in the studio right now.

## Brand Summary

Bombay Craft is a digital-first creative and technology studio building websites, digital products, CRM systems, and business automation, supported by design, branding, marketing, video, and photography. We serve small businesses and large organizations with thoughtful, practical digital solutions, and aim to build a collaborative, people-first organization whose growth can eventually support NGOs and communities in meaningful, measurable ways.

**Our guiding idea: Build useful things. Craft them with care. Grow with purpose.**
